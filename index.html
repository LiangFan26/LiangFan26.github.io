Research Interests

  • Skeleton-based Identification
  • Video analysis

My research focuses on deep learning and computer vision, particularly image and video understanding, biometric recognition, and multimodal information retrieval. I also work on AI for sports, healthcare. Through academic and industry collaborations, I have developed and applied AI systems in sports performance analysis and dairy monitoring.
